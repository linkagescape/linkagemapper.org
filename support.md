We offer dedicated, ongoing support, a hallmark of LinkageMapper that has been widely recognized (Dutta et al. 2022)[^1].

Upon encountering an error, you can peruse our short [Troubleshooting Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/13-jrIVQEb_bTHrsFx3F7aO0S2H9C6WFBww9kYx9qAKw/edit?usp=sharing) (with comments/suggested edits welcome) or use the search function in our [Community Forum](https://groups.google.com/forum/?utm_source=digest&utm_medium=email/#!forum/linkage-mapper).  

<img src="/img/google_groups_snap.jpg" style="display: block; padding:5px;" hspace="0" width="951" height="489"> 

If you are still stuck after both of these steps please post your question to the [Community Forum](https://groups.google.com/forum/?utm_source=digest&utm_medium=email/#!forum/linkage-mapper) using the tips for this that are listed in the [Troubleshooting Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/13-jrIVQEb_bTHrsFx3F7aO0S2H9C6WFBww9kYx9qAKw/edit?usp=sharing).

You can also contact with us via the email address in the Troubleshooting Doc. If you cannot open the Doc, there is a [2025 .pdf](https://drive.google.com/file/d/137Ug31jkZLXwlYO9JP03a_D2Hkq9ougI/view?usp=sharing) snapshot of it here.  

[^1]: It was great that we scored so high in the other metrics too, and that our package was the one that addressed the highest number of connectivity characteristics (Table 1).
